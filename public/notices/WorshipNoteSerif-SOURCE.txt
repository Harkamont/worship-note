Modifications for the app:
- Instance weight 600 from the variable font.
- WOFF2 subset retaining modern Hangul syllables U+AC00–U+D7A3,
  Korean jamo, Latin, common punctuation and currency characters.
- Renamed family to Worship Note Serif; font outlines were not redrawn.
- Bundled locally, without any Google Fonts network request at app runtime.

Reproduction tools: fontTools varLib.instancer and subset, Brotli.
The complete upstream font includes scripts not required by this interface.
Unavailable glyphs use the user's locally installed fallback serif font.
